Company Profile

Established electrical engineering support for critical infrastructure.

Established in 2008, RPS Switchgear SA provides electrical engineering solutions across switchgear, retrofit work, low voltage assemblies, substations, renewable energy, construction, maintenance, and electrification projects.

The company supports industrial, utility, municipal, mining, infrastructure, and energy-sector clients with practical engineering, installation, commissioning, upgrade, and maintenance capability.

Project Support

Engineering-led project support.

RPS combines product supply, technical support, field execution, and lifecycle maintenance for electrical infrastructure projects in South Africa and the broader region.
